# The Lighthouse Robot

An original short story, included as an example. Put your own stories in this folder.

## Chapter 1: The Keeper
On a cold rock at the edge of the world stood a lighthouse, and in it lived a small robot named Pip.
Pip was built from spare parts: a kettle for a head, two bicycle wheels for feet and one blue lamp for an eye.
Every evening Pip climbed ninety-nine steps to polish the great lens, because the old keeper, Mara, had grown too tired to climb.

## Chapter 2: The Storm
One night the sea turned black and the wind howled. Mara slept in her chair by the stove.
Pip saw the lights of a fishing boat tossed between the waves, far too close to the rocks.
The lamp had gone dark. A wave had cracked the glass and the oil had spilled.

Pip did not know how to fix the lamp, so it did the only thing it could. It took its own blue eye out of its head, carried it up the ninety-nine steps and set it in the lamp.
The blue light swept over the water. The boat turned, slid past the rocks and found the harbour.

## Chapter 3: Morning
At dawn the fishermen climbed the rock to say thank you. Mara woke and found Pip in the dark, unable to see.
The fishermen gave Pip a new eye made of green sea glass, and the lighthouse shone blue and green from that day on.
Pip kept climbing the ninety-nine steps every evening, and Mara never needed to climb them again.
